Input Transaction
       ↓
   Preprocess
       ↓
   Random Forest (40%) ─┐
   XGBoost (40%) ───────├─→ Weighted Vote
   Isolation Forest (20%)─┘
       ↓
   Risk = (RF×0.4) + (XGB×0.4) + (ISO×0.2)
       ↓
   IF Risk > 0.5: FRAUD ⚠️
   ELSE: LEGITIMATE ✓
       ↓
   Gemini AI: Generate Explanation
       ↓
   Return Result + Explanation
